Super Plomber 3D Privacy Policy

Your privacy, in plain terms

Effective 10 August 2026 · Applies to the iOS, iPadOS, macOS and tvOS versions

Super Plomber 3D is a single-player platformer. It has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics — and it does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information.

✓

We collect nothing. The game works entirely offline. Nothing you do in it ever leaves your device.

Information we collect

None. We do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any identifier. The game requests no special permissions and includes no third-party SDKs, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

What the game saves on your device

To remember where you left off, the game stores a few small values locally using the operating system's standard app storage. This data never leaves your device and is not readable by us or anyone else:

Level progress
Which levels you have unlocked
Personal best
Your lowest total death count
Preferences
Sound on/off and on-screen button choice

Deleting the app removes all of this data permanently.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data whatsoever, it is fully compliant with children's privacy laws such as COPPA and the GDPR-K. There is nothing to gather from a child, or from anyone else.

Third parties

There are none. The app makes no network requests, integrates no external services, and shares no data with Apple beyond the standard, aggregate information every App Store app receives (such as anonymous crash statistics you can opt out of in your device settings).

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Since the app collects no data, we do not expect meaningful changes.
